How it works

From the fridge door to the wall in three steps.

No scanning, no cropping by hand. One photo can hold a whole page of drawings.

  1. Take a photoSnap a page of artwork with your phone camera, or pick a photo you already have.
  2. Each drawing is cut outDrawing Wall finds every drawing on the page and cuts it out along its outline, like a sticker.
  3. It joins the wallYour wall grows with every photo. Move, rotate, and resize drawings to customize your wall how you want it.

Story Book

Picture books starring your child's characters.

Story Book turns drawings from the wall into a picture book. Making one takes three picks.

  1. 1
    Pick the charactersChoose 1 to 7 drawings from the wall, or add new ones from a photo.
  2. 2
    Pick a placeChoose from 20 places made up for your cast, each with its own story idea.
  3. 3
    Pick the kind of storyTreasure hunt, trip to space, bedtime and more.
  • Treasure hunt
  • Trip to space
  • Under the sea
  • Dinosaur day
  • Pirate ship
  • Surprise party
  • Lost balloon
  • Bedtime

AI writes the story for your characters, and they are placed in every scene like stickers. Drag a character to move it, change the words or the place, then save the book as a PDF to print or share.

Features

A home for every drawing.

Keep the whole collection in one place, tidy it up, and find any drawing again in seconds.

Many drawings, one photo

Photograph a full page and each drawing on it is found and cut out on its own.

Arrange it your way

Drag, turn, resize, mirror and flip any drawing. Undo and redo are always there.

Organize and collages

Tidy the wall in one tap, oldest first or by type. Save a collage and come back to it later.

Names and search

Name every drawing and mark it as a character, object or background. Find any one by its number or name.

Touch up and recrop

Erase stray marks or bring back a missed piece with a brush. Recrop from the original photo if you need to.

Read to me

Story Book reads every page aloud in your pick of natural voices. Once a voice is downloaded, it works offline.

On every device

Sign in once and your wall and books follow you to every phone and computer you use.

Save books as PDF

Save any story book as a PDF to print at home or send to grandparents.

Ready on day one

New walls start with six example drawings, so you can try Story Book before adding a single photo.

Made for families

Your child's art stays yours.

A grown-up sets up the account, and the wall is private to that account. No one but you can view your child's drawings. Read the full privacy policy.

What Story Book sends to AI

Only the characters' names and the choices you made: the place, the kind of story, your idea and the length. The drawings themselves are not sent. The story comes back into your book, and no other copy is kept.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I download Drawing Wall?

Drawing Wall is in testing on Google Play ahead of its public release. Android users can join the tester team and try it now. iPhone users can join the test through TestFlight, Apple's app for trying apps before launch.

How do I become a tester?

On Android, send us the Gmail address you use on Google Play and we'll email you the Google Play link. On iPhone, open our TestFlight link and tap Accept, no email needed. The steps are on the tester page.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes, in testing. iPhone testing is open through TestFlight, Apple's free testing app: join on iPhone, no email needed. It isn't on the App Store yet.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A parent or guardian creates one with a name, email address and password. The account keeps your wall and books safe and lets them follow you to every device you sign in on.

Where are the drawings kept?

In your private account, stored with our database provider on servers in the United States. Only your account can read them. We don't look at them unless you ask us for help.

Does the AI see my child's drawings?

No. To write a story, only the characters' names and your story choices are sent. The drawings stay out of it.

What if a drawing is missed or cut out badly?

Drawing Wall looks for the paper, whatever its color, and tries to ignore shadows and lamp light. If a piece is missed, recrop it from the original photo, or use Touch up to erase or bring back parts.

Does it cost anything?

Drawing Wall is free on Google Play, with no ads.

How do I delete our data?

In the app, tap the menu at the top right, then Delete account, and tap it again to confirm. Your account and every drawing, photo, collage and book are removed straight away. You can also email us from your account's email address.

How do I get help?

Email tarynmaloney5@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
